## Findings and changes

1. The old slice counter reset only after a passing check. A completed failing check now opens the next repair slice; policy denials do not.
2. Absolute persistent limits now stop tool use at 80 calls or after 64 assistant steps. They are reconstructed from session events and survive reloads.
3. Request output is capped at 8,192 tokens while the route continues to use `max` reasoning effort.
4. Two environment failures for one check or runtime family stop further checks. Runtime version/location probes and edits to `.pytool`, `.venv`, `node_modules`, `sitecustomize.py`, or generated runtime bootstraps are rejected.
5. Duplicate persisted `tool/call` events are deduplicated by `callId`, preventing Repeat Guard from rejecting a legitimate second attempt.
6. Probe A showed that policy denials were incorrectly consuming the diagnostic budget. They no longer do.
7. Probe B showed that the 10-call diagnostic window and directory policy were still too strict: `ls reference`, `ls tests/public`, and known deep subtree globs were rejected. The final locally verified revision raises the window to 16, permits an explicit relative subdirectory shallow listing, and permits recursive-all globs only when anchored at least two directory levels deep.

No third live probe was run, deliberately, to preserve the user's OpenCode Go token budget. The final subdirectory/window adjustment is covered by unit tests and the real Cordis fake-API smoke but remains to be confirmed in the next ordinary production session.
